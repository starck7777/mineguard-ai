# Demonstration BOM

- 600 × 400 × 350 mm acrylic/ply enclosure and soil tray
- ESP32, SX1276/78 LoRa pair, antennas
- VL53L1X, MPU6050/BNO055, ADXL345, capacitive moisture probe
- NEMA17, A4988 with heatsink, 8 mm lead screw, coupler, rails and bearings
- Two normally-closed limits, latching E-stop, buzzer, beacon LED
- Fused 12 V motor PSU and regulated logic supply
- Optional solar panel, Li-ion battery with protected charger, HX711/load cell

## Sensor-node assembly

Approximate editable dimensions are stored in `sensor_node_dimensions.json`. The node uses a 150 × 110 × 80 mm weather-resistant prototype enclosure, a 140 × 90 × 8 mm solar panel, protected 18650 holder and restraint, internal PCB tray, cable glands, external LoRa antenna, 120 mm ground-coupled sensor plate, 300 mm pointed stake and 160 mm capacitive probe. Do not infer a certified IP rating from this prototype selection.

See `sensor-node-exploded.svg` for the printable numbered assembly drawing.
